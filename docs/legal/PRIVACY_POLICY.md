## What we collect

- Account: display name, optional graduation year, optional avatar.
- Verification: a school email (stored privately, never shown publicly) used only
  to confirm school membership.
- Content you create: listings, offers, messages, events, community posts.
- Technical: device push tokens, minimal logs needed for security and delivery.

## What we do not do

- We do not display your email or phone number publicly.
- We do not sell personal data or run advertising.
- We do not track precise location.

## How we use data

To operate school-scoped features, verify membership, deliver notifications, and
keep the community safe (moderation, abuse prevention).
