SWAP! is a private, invitation-only, school-scoped app for verified students to give,
swap, borrow, and lend items with classmates. This policy explains what we collect,
why, and your choices.

## Who this is for; age

SWAP! is for students **aged 13 and older** who have been invited by a participating
school. We do **not** knowingly create accounts for anyone under 13. We confirm you
are 13+ before you use the app, and we do **not** collect a date of birth.

## What we collect

- **Account:** your display name, an optional graduation year, and an optional avatar.
- **School verification:** a school email used only to confirm you belong to the
  school. It is stored **privately (hashed/normalized), never shown to other users**,
  and never used for advertising.
- **Content you create:** listings, offers, in-app messages, wishlists, community
  posts, reports you file, and photos you attach to listings.
- **Technical:** minimal logs needed for security, abuse prevention, and reliability.

## What we do NOT collect or do

- **No** precise or coarse location. **No** phone number or home address required.
- **No** advertising, **no** ad identifiers, **no** third-party ad/analytics
  tracking, and we do **not** sell or rent personal data.
- **No** payment or financial data (the app takes no payments).
- We do **not** display your email publicly.

## How we use data

To operate the school-scoped marketplace and community, verify membership, show your
content to verified students at **your** school (and not other schools), enable safe
in-person handoffs, and keep the community safe through reporting and human
moderation.

## Who we share it with

- **Other students at your school** see the content you choose to post (not your
  email).
- **Service providers (subprocessors)** that host the app process data on our behalf
  under contract — e.g. our cloud backend/database and image storage provider
  (Supabase) and, once enabled, an email provider used only to send verification
  messages. They may not use your data for their own purposes.
- **Your school's moderators/administrators** may review reported content to enforce
  the guidelines.
- **Legal/safety:** we may disclose information if required by law or to prevent harm.

We do **not** share data with advertisers or data brokers.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access is constrained by per-school
database row-level security so one school cannot see another's data. School emails
are stored hashed in a private schema.

## Your choices & rights

In **Settings → Account & privacy** you can:

- **Edit** your profile,
- **Download** a copy of your data, and
- **Delete** your account.

Deletion is reversible for a short window, then your personal profile is scrubbed
(anonymized). Certain records required for completed exchanges, safety reports, and
moderation are retained in de-identified form. See
[../ACCOUNT_DELETION_AND_RETENTION.md](../ACCOUNT_DELETION_AND_RETENTION.md) and
[../privacy-data-retention.md](../privacy-data-retention.md).

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (access, correction,
deletion, objection). Contact us to exercise them.
