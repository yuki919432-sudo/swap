Community Guidelines

SWAP! exists to reduce waste, help students reuse items, support student-led activities, and build a stronger, safer school community.

Do

  • Be honest about item condition and intentions.
  • Be respectful and kind to other members.
  • Meet at recommended safe, on-campus locations for handoffs.
  • Report anything unsafe or inappropriate.

Don't

  • Don't post prohibited items (weapons, drugs, alcohol, nicotine, stolen or counterfeit goods, prescription medication, sexually explicit content, dangerous materials, illegal services).
  • Don't harass, bully, scam, spam, or mislead.
  • Don't share others' private information, or your own precise home address.
  • Don't try to contact or trade with people outside your school through the app.

Safety

You can block users and report content, users, messages, and events. Administrators review reports and can remove content or suspend members. Reports of objectionable content or abusive behavior are reviewed by human moderators, who aim to act within 24 hours.

Violations may result in content removal, warnings, suspension, or removal from the school community.
