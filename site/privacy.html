Privacy Policy

Effective date: [to be set at launch]

SWAP! is a private, invitation-only, school-scoped app for verified students to give, swap, borrow, and lend items with classmates. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Who this is for; age

SWAP! is for students aged 13 and older who have been invited by a participating school. We do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 13. We confirm you are 13+ before you use the app, and we do not collect a date of birth.

What we collect

  • Account: your display name, an optional graduation year, and an optional avatar.
  • School verification: a school email used only to confirm you belong to the school. It is stored privately (hashed), is never shown to other users, and is never used for advertising.
  • Content you create: listings, offers, in-app messages, wishlists, community posts, reports you file, and photos you attach to listings.
  • Technical: minimal logs needed for security, abuse prevention, and reliability.

What we do NOT collect or do

  • No precise or coarse location. No phone number or home address required.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no third-party ad/analytics tracking. We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • No payment or financial data (the app takes no payments).
  • We do not display your email publicly.

How we use data

To operate the school-scoped marketplace and community, verify membership, show your content to verified students at your school (and not other schools), enable safe in-person handoffs, and keep the community safe through reporting and human moderation.

Who we share it with

  • Other students at your school see the content you choose to post (not your email).
  • Service providers that host the app process data on our behalf under contract (e.g. our cloud database/storage provider, and an email provider used only for verification messages). They may not use your data for their own purposes.
  • Your school's moderators/administrators may review reported content to enforce the guidelines.
  • Legal/safety: we may disclose information if required by law or to prevent harm.

We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access is constrained by per-school database row-level security so one school cannot see another's data. School emails are stored hashed in a private schema.

Your choices & rights

In Settings → Account & privacy you can edit your profile, download a copy of your data, and delete your account. Deletion is reversible for a short window, then your personal profile is anonymized; certain records required for completed exchanges, safety reports, and moderation are retained in de-identified form. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (access, correction, deletion, objection); contact us to exercise them.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be communicated in-app.
