Support

Need help with SWAP!, or want to report a problem or a safety concern? We're here to help.

Contact

Email us at [support email to be set]. We aim to respond within a few business days.

Reporting content or people

Inside the app, tap the ••• menu on any listing, message, or profile and choose Report. Reports go to your school's moderators. You can also block a user from a conversation, and manage blocked users in Settings.

If someone is in danger

SWAP! is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your school staff or local emergency services.

Your account & data

In Settings → Account & privacy you can edit your profile, download a copy of your data, or delete your account.
